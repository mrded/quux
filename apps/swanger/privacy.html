Swanger – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What data the app uses

Swanger reads OpenAPI files from your Mac. It never sends those files — or anything else — over the network.

What data we collect

None. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, and no accounts.

Try it drafts

JSON payloads you type in the Try it tab are saved locally in your Mac's preferences (UserDefaults) and never leave your device.

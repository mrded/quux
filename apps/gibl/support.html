Gibl – Support

Need help with Gibl? We're happy to help.

Common questions

Why is the app called Beer Notes on my phone?
Beer Notes is the name Gibl uses on your home screen. It's the same app.

Where are the beers from the App Store version?
The beta doesn't import them yet, so it starts with an empty list. It leaves your old data untouched so a later release can bring it across.

My beers aren't syncing.
Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account, with iCloud Drive on and an active internet connection. While iCloud is off, new beers are saved on the device and move to iCloud when it's turned back on.

I can't see the flavour chart.
The chart appears once at least three flavours are scored above zero. Open the beer and tap Review to score them.

How do I delete a beer?
Swipe left on it in the list and tap Delete.

Gibl – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers Gibl 3, the current TestFlight beta. On your device, the app appears as Beer Notes.

What data the app uses

Gibl stores the beers you log: their names, breweries, styles, notes, ratings, flavour scores and photos. This data is saved in your personal iCloud account or, if iCloud is off, on your device. The developer has no access to this data.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our own servers. The app has no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is on, your beers sync between your devices using iCloud Drive. They are kept in the app's own folder, hidden from the Files app. This sync is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a beer. The photo you pick is saved with that beer.

TestFlight

Apple runs TestFlight and may share beta usage information with us, such as installs, sessions and crash reports, along with any feedback you send through TestFlight.

Feedback

The Feedback option opens your email app. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself, and we only use it to reply to you.

Tit for Tat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What data the app uses

Tit for Tat stores your duty log entries and sharing preferences. All data is saved exclusively in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. The developer has no access to this data.

What data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our own servers. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, and no third-party SDKs.

iCloud sync

If you choose to share the app with your partner, data is synced privately between your iCloud accounts using CloudKit. This sync is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The app may send local notifications as daily reminders. These are scheduled on-device and no notification data leaves your phone.

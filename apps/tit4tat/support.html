Need help with Tit for Tat? We're happy to help.

Common questions

How do I invite the other parent?
Open the Family tab and tap Invite. They'll receive an iCloud sharing invitation.

My data isn't syncing.
Make sure both devices are signed into iCloud and have an active internet connection. Pull down to refresh.

How do I reset the app?
Go to Settings inside the app and tap Reset. This removes all local data.
